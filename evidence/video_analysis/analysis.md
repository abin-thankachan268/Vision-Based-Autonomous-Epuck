# Simulation Recording Analysis

Source: `supporting_materials/Interactive_Simulation_Recording.mp4`

- Duration: 61.184 seconds
- Resolution: 640 x 480
- Frame rate: 31.25 frames/s
- Frames: 1,912

The extracted overview and detailed contact sheets in this folder were used to
inspect the red obstacle at 0-10 seconds and the two blue crossings later in the
lap. The red contact was caused by a late proximity response that weakened the
camera S-curve before its emergency threshold. The second blue contact was a
rear/flank sensing gap compounded by two synchronized obstacles travelling in
opposite directions.

The corrected interactive system strengthens the frontal turn, monitors all
eight proximity sectors, provides rear escape, aligns the constantly moving blue
objects in the same ping-pong direction and waits for 24 clear camera frames.

Final result: two consecutive completed passes, zero contacts under a 0.090 m
conservative envelope, 0.090483 m minimum static clearance and 0.091130 m
minimum moving clearance. The user's saved WBT viewpoint was not changed.
